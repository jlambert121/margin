# A little room to think.

Welcome to **Margin**. A quiet place for your notes, ideas, and everything in between.

## Just start writing

Your words take shape as you type. Use Markdown shortcuts for headings, **bold**, *italics*, and lists — the formatting appears right here on the page.

> The secret of getting ahead is getting started.

## Make yourself at home

- Open a Markdown file with **⌘O**
- Browse a folder with **⇧⌘O**
- Switch to source mode with **⌘/**
- Save your work with **⌘S**
